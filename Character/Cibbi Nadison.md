# - Introduction 
Cibbi is a magician who owned can can control sky crystal magic. She is be able to control wind, weather, thunder, teleportation, and make her own realm. She is mostly introverted and loves spend her time to study, reads. and writes books either in library or her own realm. She mostly shows flat expression when she is with another people.

When she was young, her parents were dead from housefire. She was adopted by Nartial Kingdom family because of her intelligence, got her sky magic crystal and became the sky princess. After the collapse of kingdom system and rise of presidential system because of coup, she become a member of a criminal organization disguising as academic institution to tell the truth of the end kingdom system history that presidential system government hide.

#  - Lore
She was born in January 13th in [[Nizema]], Nartial. Live as the only child in the family. She got great amount love from her parent. At this time, she mostly spends her time to play with neighbor kid or reading fiction books in her bedroom just like typical kid. Also she is labeled as smart student because she always got 1st rank at school.

When she was 10 years old, there was a gas leakage in her house. At that time, she was watering plants at her home. Everyone was trying to let the gas out of the house, but one of her house member switched the light on cluelessly. The house was burned and her parents and grandparents inside that home was dead. 

After her family death, now she become the only pink cat that lives in Nartial. The only thing that left  were [[pink cat crystal]] (a magic crystal that allows her to transform) and her mom's cardigan.

Then she was lived in orphanage. Still be the smart student, she always get extras which is weird because why she must get the extras.

After few months, the king made a controversial decision to adopted 1 orphan per big island that exist, total of 3. Cibbi was one of the orphan that chosen. She was given the [[sky crystal]], a power to control wind and thunder. She wondered why she was the one who got wind, even though there was other orphan who were 1 year older than her. The king explained that he chose her because of her intelligence. In the end she accepted the sky crystal and became the [[Sky Princess]].
